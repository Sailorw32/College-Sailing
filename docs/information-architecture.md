# Information architecture

The current site groups content by internal department (About, Racing,
Championships, Hall of Fame, Media, Resources, SVSH Task Force, Conferences,
Scores — 10 top-level items). This rebuild groups by **who's visiting**
instead, per the audit's recommendation.

## Primary navigation (this pass)

- **Racing** — schedules, rankings, championships. Merges the current
  site's separate "Racing" and "Championships" menus, since visitors don't
  distinguish between them.
- **Recruits & Families** — new section. What college sailing is, how
  recruiting works, links to team pages. The current site has no equivalent;
  everything relevant is scattered across About/Resources.
- **Hall of Fame**
- **News**
- **Resources** — kept distinct: documents, meeting minutes, forms. This
  bucket is for coaches and conference administrators, not recruits, and
  mixing the two audiences was the original site's core IA problem.
- **About** — governance, conferences, contact.

## Deferred to later passes

- Full sub-navigation for Racing (live schedules, TechScore/Scores
  integration) and Resources (the current 10-item fan-out) needs real
  content ownership decisions from ICSA, not just a template.
- SVSH Task Force: kept reachable from About for now; worth a dedicated
  audit of whether it needs its own top-level placement.
- Conference-specific navigation: this pass only builds the national site.
  Bringing MAISA/NEISA/etc. onto the same system is roadmap Phase 3.

## Principle for future sections

Before adding a new top-level nav item, ask which of the three audiences
(recruits & families / competitors & coaches / conference administrators)
it serves. If a proposed page serves more than one, it likely belongs in
more than one place (cross-linked), not duplicated as a new top-level menu.
